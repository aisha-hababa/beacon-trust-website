About Us

Close to the people we serve.

Beacon Trust was founded by a small group of neighbours who refused to look away. Today we are a network of social workers, teachers and volunteers standing by families through difficult times.

Education

Free tutoring and learning mentorships for children from low-income families.

Guidance

Personal support points for families facing financial and social hardship.

Community

Open meeting spaces, festivals and projects that bring neighbourhoods back together.
